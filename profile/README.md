<h1 align="center">GekkGo</h1>

<p align="center">Know what every drive costs.</p>

We are building a navigation app for drivers in the UAE. It works out what a trip costs before you leave, with fuel for your exact car, Salik and Darb tolls and parking, then guides you along the route you chose and warns you about the things that matter on UAE roads: speed limits, cameras, school zones, bumps and weather.

The app is an Android prototype today, with Android Auto support for cars with a rotary knob or buttons. A Wear OS companion is planned.
